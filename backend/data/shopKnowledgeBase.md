# VoxLead Electronics Showroom — Knowledge Base

## Store Overview and Hours
VoxLead Electronics is a multi-brand flagship electronics showroom specializing in Smartphones, Laptops, Televisions, Headphones and Audio, Smartwatches, and Accessories.
Showroom Timings: Open all 7 days of the week from 10:00 AM to 9:00 PM (including Sundays and most public holidays).
Location: Tech Plaza Ground Floor, MG Road, Bengaluru. Free basement customer parking is available for 2 hours with any showroom visit.

## Warranty and VoxCare Extended Protection
All brand-new products come with the official 1-year manufacturer warranty (or 2 years on select laptops and televisions as listed in the catalog).
Customers can also opt for VoxCare Extended Protection at checkout, which adds 1 or 2 extra years of hardware coverage plus accidental liquid and screen damage protection starting at ₹1,499 depending on device price.
Warranty claims can be initiated directly at our showroom service desk with the original tax invoice.

## Return, Exchange, and Refund Policy
VoxLead Electronics offers a 7-day easy replacement policy for manufacturing defects or Dead-on-Arrival (DOA) items, provided the original box, accessories, and invoice are intact.
Unopened, sealed items can be returned or exchanged within 7 days for a full refund or store credit.
Once opened and activated, non-defective items are eligible for store exchange within 48 hours subject to a physical inspection by our showroom technician.
Refunds to UPI or bank cards are processed within 3 to 5 business days.

## Payment Methods, No-Cost EMI, and Discounts
We accept UPI, Debit/Credit Cards, Net Banking, Cash (up to ₹1,00,000), and Bajaj Finserv / HDFC / ICICI / SBI Card EMIs.
No-Cost EMI is available for 3, 6, 9, and 12 months on purchases above ₹15,000 with major credit cards.
Students with a valid college ID receive an extra 5% instant discount on laptops and tablets. Corporate GST billing is available for business buyers.

## Delivery, Store Pickup, and Free Setup
In-stock products can be picked up immediately at the showroom or delivered the same day within city limits for orders placed before 4:00 PM.
Same-day local delivery is free on orders above ₹10,000.
Every laptop and smartphone purchase includes complimentary unboxing, OS updates, and data transfer setup by our showroom specialists.

## Trade-In and Device Exchange Program
Customers can trade in old smartphones, laptops, or smartwatches for instant exchange value toward a new purchase.
Our showroom specialist inspects the old device in 10 minutes and applies the exchange discount immediately on the final invoice.
Bring the device, a valid government ID, and original charger if available for the highest trade-in value.

## Customer Support and Showroom Appointments
Customers can book a personal 1-on-1 showroom demo or consultation slot anytime through our voice agent by sharing their preferred date, time, name, and phone number.
If a customer needs custom bulk pricing, corporate quotes, or escalated support, our voice agent can log a human representative handoff request immediately.
